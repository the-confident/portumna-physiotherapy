About the Clinic

Brian Murphy MISCP
Chartered Physiotherapist

Portumna Physiotherapy & Sports Injury Clinic was established in July 2002 and has been providing expert musculoskeletal and sports injury care to patients across East Galway and the wider Midlands region ever since.

Brian places particular emphasis on accurate diagnosis, thorough patient education, and evidence-based treatment.

Portumna Physiotherapy & Sports Injury Clinic is a custom-built, fully wheelchair-accessible facility with private parking. It is well equipped with 2 private treatment rooms, a large gym area, a waiting room, and a toilet.

Patients of all ages are welcomed, from children with developmental conditions to older adults managing long-term musculoskeletal issues.

Conditions Treated

We treat a wide range of conditions

Whether you’re dealing with acute back pain, chronic arthritis, or post-surgical rehabilitation, you will find excellent care here. Brian’s approach begins with a thorough assessment to establish an accurate diagnosis before establishing an effective, personalised treatment plan.

  • Back & Neck pain
  • Nerve pain e.g. sciatica, carpal tunnel, etc
  • Hip, knee, ankle & foot problems
  • Shoulder, elbow, wrist and hand problems
  • Sports Injuries
  • Arthritic & Rheumatic pathologies
  • Postural & Ergonomic advice
  • Musculoskeletal problems – muscle strains & ligament sprains
  • Pre & Post surgical treatment
  • Repetitive strain injuries
  • Neurological disorders
  • Whiplash, headaches, dizziness, migraines, sinusitis, hayfever, etc
